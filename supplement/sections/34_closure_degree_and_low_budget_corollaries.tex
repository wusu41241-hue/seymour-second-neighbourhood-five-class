\section{Degree localization and an arbitrary-order low-far-budget class}
\label{sec:closure-degree-low-budget-corollaries}

Write $h_D(v)$ for the number of originally protected far heads of $v$,
and $t_D(v)$ for its originally unprotected far heads. In every finite
oriented graph of order $n$,
\[
 t_D(v)-g_D(v)=n-1-2d_D^+(v)-h_D(v).
\]
This follows by partitioning all other vertices into exact first,
exact second and far targets, and splitting the far targets into
protected and unprotected heads.

\begin{corollary}[Adjusted-degree localization]
\label{cor:closure-adjusted-degree-localization}
At every median feed $f$ of the iterated closure maximizing completion
with $g_D(f)>0$,
\[
 2d_D^+(f)+h_D(f)\le n-2.
\]
If $\delta=\delta^+(D)$ and $q=n-2\delta$, then
\[
 0\le d_D^+(f)-\delta
     \le\left\lfloor\frac{q-2-h_D(f)}2\right\rfloor.
\]
In particular when $n=2\delta+3$, every such feed has
$d_D^+(f)=\delta$ and $h_D(f)\le1$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The strict original surplus gives $t_D(f)-g_D(f)\ge1$.
Substitute the identity and rearrange. The last assertion uses that
the nonnegative integer $d_D^+(f)-\delta$ is at most
$\lfloor(1-h_D(f))/2\rfloor$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[At most one unprotected far head at each vertex]
\label{cor:closure-all-low-far-heads-snc}
Every finite nonempty oriented graph with $t_D(v)\le1$ at every vertex
has a Seymour vertex. No graph-order, minimality, missing-pair or
cross-witness-flexibility hypothesis is required.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
If all vertices were deficient, any feed of the iterated-closure
maximizing completion would have $t_D(f)\ge g_D(f)+1\ge2$.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Scope.}
These statements are consequences of the all-order closure proof, not
finite enumeration. The degree localization is conditional on original
positive gap at the chosen feed. It does not assert that every graph
of order $2\delta+3$ has a minimum-outdegree Seymour vertex.
Novelty relative to the extensive existing SNC literature remains to
be assessed.
