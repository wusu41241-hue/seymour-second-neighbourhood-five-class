\section{An arbitrary-order one-exception completion theorem}
\label{sec:one-exception-short-proof}

All predicates refer to the original finite nonempty oriented graph $D$.
Let $A$ be its actual bad-direction failure sources, $\mathcal F(x)$
its unprotected far targets and $t(x)=|\mathcal F(x)|$. Protection is
$y\triangleleft x$ if $y\to x$ and
$N_D^-(y)\subseteq N_D^-(x)\setminus\{y\}$.
A missing pair is flexible if it is bad or is good with both directions
convenient. An opposite pair of bad-direction witnesses is an original
missing cross-witness pair.

\begin{theorem}[At most one uncontrolled actual source]
\label{thm:one-exception-short-general}
Suppose a vertex $u$ satisfies:
\begin{enumerate}
\item every source $x\in A\setminus\{u\}$ has $t(x)\le1$;
\item every cross-witness pair between two sources in
$A\setminus\{u\}$ is flexible.
\end{enumerate}
Then $D$ has a Seymour vertex. There is no condition on the budgets
of inactive vertices, and no bound on the order or source support.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Assume $g_D(x)>0$ for every vertex. For \emph{every} linear extension
$\sigma$ of protection, let $T_\sigma$ complete every flexible pair
forward in $\sigma$ and keep every uniquely convenient good direction
fixed. The universal reference-priority envelope preserves every
original protected far pair and gives
\[
 g_{T_\sigma}(x)\ge g_D(x)+2|K_x|-t(x),\qquad
 K_x=N_{T_\sigma}^+(x)\setminus N_D^+(x).
 \tag{SX1}\label{eq:one-exception-short-envelope}
\]
Any global median order of such a tournament extends original
protection: if $y\triangleleft x$ were reversed in the order, swapping
their positions gains their mutual arc and loses none of the intervening
pair contributions, by tournament domination.

Among all pairs $(T_\sigma,L)$, with $L$ any vertex order, first
maximize the unweighted forward-arc score $\Phi$ and then maximize
$\operatorname{pos}_L(u)$. The selected $L$ is a global median order
of the selected tournament $T$. Every flexible added arc is forward
in every global median order of $T$: otherwise use that median order
as a new reference, which is allowed because the family contains
\emph{every} protection extension. All flexible backward pairs become
forward, while fixed directions and original arcs are unchanged,
strictly improving $\Phi$. Let $f$ be the chosen feed.

If $f\notin A$, reverse all added outgoing feed arcs inward. This
keeps the same order global median: its score increases by the number
of reversals and every competing score increases by at most that
number. Its first neighborhood becomes original. No new far second
head can occur through an original first step: a good second direction
is convenient and a bad second direction would fail at $f\notin A$.
Its tournament feed SNP therefore transfers to the originally deficient
vertex, a contradiction.

Suppose $f\in A\setminus\{u\}$. The envelope and $t(f)\le1$ force
$K_f=\varnothing$, since any added first neighbor gives a strictly
positive completed gap. Original exact second neighbors then persist.
Feed SNP, positive original gap and the envelope force
\[
 g_D(f)=t(f)=1,\qquad g_T(f)=0,
 \qquad N_T^{++}(f)=N_D^{++}(f)\cup\{y\},
\]
where $y$ is its sole unprotected original far head. The strong feed
theorem gives $|N_T^+(f)|\le|G_L|\le|N_T^{++}(f)|$; equality of the
two neighborhoods therefore yields $G_L=N_T^{++}(f)$.

The new head $y$ has a path $f\to a\to y$ with original first arc
and added bad second arc. Choose an opposite witness $r$. Then
$r\to y$ is original, so $r\ne y$, and $fr$ is originally missing.
Since a missing far target is unprotected and $y$ is the only such
head at $f$, $r\in N_D^{++}(f)\subseteq G_L$.

Global sedimentation remains a median order and puts $f$ before every
vertex of $G_L$. If $r\ne u$, condition~2 makes $fr$ flexible; its
direction was $r\to f$ because $K_f=\varnothing$. Sedimentation
makes it backward in another median order of $T$, contradicting the
maximum-score argument. If $r=u$, sedimentation strictly increases
the position of $u$ while preserving the score, because for every
$z\in N_T^+(f)\cup G_L$ its position increases by
\[
 1+|\{b\in B_L:\operatorname{pos}_L(b)>
                            \operatorname{pos}_L(z)\}|>0,
\]
where $B_L$ is the set of bad vertices of the median order.
This contradicts the secondary maximum. Both cases are impossible.

Thus the \emph{selected} median feed is $u$. If $u\notin A$, the
outside-source transfer already gives a contradiction. Otherwise the
last position at $u$ shows that it has no original protection successor:
there is no $q$ with $u\triangleleft q$, since every median order
extends protection.

Apply the fixed-completion heavy-cover criterion
(Theorem~\ref{thm:protected-independent-heavy-cover}) with
$H=\{u\}$. Its independence condition is automatic; all other actual
sources have $t\le1$, and the absence of a protection successor at $u$
supplies its final condition. It contradicts all-deficiency.
The second phase uses a different fixed completion solely to apply
that criterion; no numerical gap or optimal score is transferred
between the completions.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Two nonunit actual failure sources]
\label{cor:one-exception-short-two-heavy}
In every finite nonempty strongly connected all-deficient oriented
graph minimal under arbitrary nonempty arc-set deletion, at least two
distinct actual bad-direction failure sources have $\eta\ge2$.
Consequently every positive residual allocation $(m,1^k)$ is excluded
for arbitrary $m\ge2$ and arbitrary finite $k\ge0$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Actual sources have positive residual, and a source of residual one
has $t\le1$. The unit--unit cross-witness-tail lemma says
that opposite unit sources have a bad, hence flexible, original missing
pair. If at most one actual source were nonunit, choose it as $u$,
or choose any vertex when none exists. The general theorem applies,
a contradiction. Residuals of inactive vertices are unrestricted.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Proof status.}
The first phase claims the feed property only for its selected
score/position optimum, not for every median order. Closure under
all protection extensions is explicit and essential. The fixed-cover
criterion has a separate proof, so the theorem and corollary have no
circular dependency. The remaining multiple-exception classes are
not excluded, and no proof of the full SNC is asserted.
