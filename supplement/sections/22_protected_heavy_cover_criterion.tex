\section{A fixed-completion criterion for an independent heavy cover}
\label{sec:protected-heavy-cover-criterion}

All graph predicates in this section refer to the original finite
nonempty oriented graph $D$. Let $A$ be its actual bad-direction
failure sources, and let $B$ be the cross-witness graph on $A$:
the two directional failure sets of each bad pair span their complete
bipartite cross-witness graph. Write $t(x)$ for the number of
unprotected original far heads of $x$.

\begin{theorem}[An independent heavy cover without protection successors]
\label{thm:protected-independent-heavy-cover}
Suppose there is a nonempty set $H$ of vertices such that:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Every source $f\in A\setminus H$ satisfies $t(f)\le1$.
\item $B[H\cap A]$ is independent.
\item No $h\in H$ is a protected far target of any source
$f\in A\setminus H$; equivalently, no original relation
$h\triangleleft f$ occurs between those sets.
\end{enumerate}
Then $D$ has a Seymour vertex. No source-support, order, residual, or
far-budget bound is imposed on $H$ or on inactive vertices.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Suppose all original gaps are positive. Independence allows each bad
pair to be oriented so that all its selected directional failures
avoid $H$: its two failure sets cannot both meet $H$, since such
opposite witnesses would give an edge of $B[H\cap A]$. Complete good
pairs canonically by any fixed reference extension of protection.
Call the resulting fixed tournament $T$. Every selected bad failure
source has $t\le1$, so the simultaneous safe envelope applies at
every vertex:
\[
 N_T^{++}(x)\subseteq
 (N_D^{++}(x)\setminus K_x)\cup
 (\mathcal F_D(x)\setminus K_x),\qquad
 g_T(x)\ge g_D(x)+2|K_x|-t(x).
\]
Among all global median orders of this one fixed tournament, choose
$L$ maximizing $\sum_{h\in H}\operatorname{pos}_L(h)$, and let $f$
be its feed.

If $f\notin A$, or if $f\in H$, reverse every added outgoing feed
arc inward. The order remains global median, its first neighborhood
is original, and it has no new far second head: good directions are
convenient, and no selected bad direction fails at $f$. Feed SNP
would transfer to the original deficient vertex, a contradiction.

Thus $f\in A\setminus H$ and $t(f)\le1$. The envelope and feed SNP
force $K_f=\varnothing$, $g_D(f)=t(f)=1$ and $g_T(f)=0$.
Writing $y$ for its unique unprotected original far head, the strong
feed theorem then gives
\[
 G_L=N_T^{++}(f)=N_D^{++}(f)\cup\{y\},\qquad
 |N_T^+(f)|=|G_L|.
\]
For every $h\in H$, condition~3 excludes original protected farness
from $f$. Since $h\ne f$, it is therefore an original first neighbor,
an original exact second neighbor, or the sole unprotected original
far head $y$. In all three cases
\[
 H\subseteq N_T^+(f)\cup G_L.
\]
Global sedimentation remains a median order and strictly increases
the position of each vertex of this union, by the exact positional
identity
\[
 \operatorname{pos}_{\mathrm{Sed}(L)}(h)-\operatorname{pos}_L(h)
 =1+|\{b\in B_L:\operatorname{pos}_L(b)>
                         \operatorname{pos}_L(h)\}|>0,
\]
where $B_L=V(T)\setminus(N_T^+(f)\cup G_L\cup\{f\})$ denotes the
\emph{bad vertices of the median order}, not the cross-witness graph.
It therefore increases the chosen sum by at least $|H|>0$, a
contradiction. This proves the criterion.
\end{proof}

The strong feed inequality and the global sedimentation assertion are
the unweighted results of Havet--Thomasse
\cite[Theorem~1 and Lemma~1]{HavetThomasse2000}; the exact positional
identity follows by counting the moved bad vertices.

\begin{corollary}[Simplifying the second phase of the one-exception proof]
\label{cor:one-heavy-second-phase-fixed}
Once the unrestricted maximum argument has proved that a unique
exceptional actual source $u$ has no original protection successor,
the second phase needs only an arbitrary completion avoiding all
failures at $u$, and a median order of that fixed completion maximizing
the position of $u$. No second family maximum or fixed-tail case
analysis is necessary.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Apply the theorem with $H=\{u\}$. Independence is automatic;
the other sources have $t\le1$, and the first phase supplied
condition~3. The proof uses only the simultaneous envelope in the
fixed completion and its global sedimentation operation.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[A global alternative for minimal counterexamples]
\label{cor:heavy-heavy-edge-or-protected-unit}
In the strong arbitrary-arc-set-deletion-minimal all-deficient class,
put $H=\{h\in A:\eta(h)\ge2\}$. The unit closure makes
$H$ nonempty. A counterexample must have either:
\begin{enumerate}
\item a cross-witness edge joining two vertices of $H$; or
\item an actual unit source $f$ and an $h\in H$ with
$h\triangleleft f$ in original protection.
\end{enumerate}
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
If neither occurs, all hypotheses of the fixed-completion criterion
hold. Actual sources outside $H$ have residual one and hence $t\le1$.
The criterion contradicts all-deficiency.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Audit boundary.}
The theorem does not show that every heavy cover is independent or
that its protection-successor condition always holds. It exhibits
the specific two global obstructions that remain after unit budgets
are controlled. Its proof uses one fixed tournament, not a comparison
of numerical gaps from different completions. Ordinary two-Seymour
vertex theorems are not used, and global median orders, not merely
local median orders, are essential.

\paragraph{A local protection-obstruction control.}
The ten-vertex out masks
\[
 [540,541,96,96,96,1,3,28,128,256]
\]
have an actual unit source $f=0$ with
$g(f)=\eta(f)=t(f)=1$ and
$\partial P(f)=N_D^{--}(f)=\{2,3,4\}$.
The actual source $h=1$ has $g(h)=2$, $\eta(h)=2$, $t(h)=1$, and
$h\triangleleft f$, because
$N_D^-(h)=\{6\}\subseteq N_D^-(f)\setminus\{h\}=\{5,6\}$.
The bad missing pair $\{7,9\}$ has failure sources $\{0,1\}$ for
$9\to7$ and $\{8\}$ for $7\to9$. Several other vertices have
nonpositive gap, so this is not an SNC counterexample and does not
meet the complete minimal-counterexample hypotheses. It does show
that a heavy-protecting-unit relation is not contradicted by the unit's
local residual, positive gap and full packet alone.
The independent program
\texttt{audit\_heavy\_protects\_unit\_control.js} verifies all these claims.
