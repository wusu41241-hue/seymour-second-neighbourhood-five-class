\section{An even-order capacity identity}
For any oriented graph $H$, set
$b_H(v)=d_H^+(v)-1-d_H^{++}(v)$ and $B(H)=\sum_v b_H(v)$.
These quantities can be negative in a vertex-deleted graph.
At order $n'=2\alpha+2$ with actual minimum outdegree $\alpha$,
write $q'(x)=|T'(x)|-\alpha$ and define the capacity surplus
$\sigma'(x)=2q'(x)-1-|P'(x)|$ when $q'>0$, and zero when $q'=0$.
Let $z'$ count whole vertices of outdegree $\alpha$.
Then
\[
 B(H)+n'=z'-\sum_x\sigma'(x).
\]
Indeed $q'=0$ implies $P'=\varnothing$ by the same capacity count;
these vertices are exactly the $z'$ perfect vertices. Summing
$q'$ gives $n'(\alpha+1)-\sum d^+$, while summing the far-in sets
gives $n'(n'-1)-\sum d^+-\sum d^{++}$. Substitution proves the identity.
No counterexample hypothesis on $H$ is used.
