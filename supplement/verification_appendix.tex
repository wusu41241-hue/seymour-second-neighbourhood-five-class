\section{Historical computations and source attribution}
The formal claims of this manuscript are the proved statements in the
article and this supplement. Earlier bounded searches, solver timeouts,
and local branch logs are indexed in the accompanying coverage inventory.
The archived minimum-degree-seven work contains two specified branches,
with 30 and 610 leaf records respectively; these counts do not imply a
proof of the entire minimum-degree-seven case. Solver logs and reproducible
source are not automatically formal unsatisfiability proof objects.

An imported excess-measurement manuscript attributed to Daiki Tahara is
external related material. Its definitions and search reports are not
presented as original theorems of this article. Likewise, unverified
repository and preprint claims from the exploratory notes are not used as
unproved premises in the present proof chain. The source-restricted
deletion identity needed for the dense-boundary predecessor estimates is
derived explicitly in its own section.

The whole conjecture remains unresolved by this work. In particular,
strictly negative completed gaps, universal pattern-certificate existence,
and arbitrary compatible heavy-source choices are open steps in these
arguments. Exact control graphs verify failures of stronger shortcuts;
each such graph has Seymour vertices elsewhere and is not a counterexample.
