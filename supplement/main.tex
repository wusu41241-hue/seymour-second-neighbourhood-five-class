\begin{abstract}
This supplement provides the original deletion and partner estimates,
the complete residual exclusions used in the manuscript, the additional
closure and transport arguments, and the separately scoped dense odd-order
tools. It records the finite verification contract and the status of earlier
computational branches. All second neighbourhoods are exact. No full solution
of Seymour's conjecture is claimed.
\end{abstract}
