Precise theorem list suitable for the manuscript
================================================
Throughout, D is a finite nonempty oriented graph without loops or digons;
N++ denotes vertices at directed distance exactly two, and g=d+-d++.

T1. Pattern-certificate transfer (unconditional).
Let C={c_1,...,c_k}, let H=D[C], put P_D(v)={i:c_i->v}, and let
P(H)={P:P is a subset of N_H^-(j) for some j}. Define
R_H(P)={i not in P:N_H^+(i) intersects P}.
Suppose every P_D(v) belongs to P(H). If a nonzero nonnegative vector lambda
satisfies lambda(R_H(P))>=lambda(P) for every P in P(H), its zero extension
has N_D^T lambda>=0, where N_D has -1 on first and +1 on exact second targets.
Consequently an original vertex c_i with lambda_i>0 has g_D(c_i)<=0.

T2. Tournament core certificate (unconditional).
If H is a tournament, every normalized nonnegative vector satisfying
(A_H-A_H^T)lambda>=0 satisfies all the inequalities of T1. Such a vector
exists by the finite skew-symmetric zero-sum game argument. Thus an
incoming-covered tournament core, including tournament representatives of
all maximal incoming classes, contains an original Seymour vertex.

T3. At most five maximal incoming classes (computer-assisted).
Partition V(D) by equality of the complete original incoming neighborhoods;
order these classes by containment of their common incoming sets. If there
are at most five maximal classes, at least one maximal class consists entirely
of Seymour vertices. Total order and all class sizes are unrestricted.
The stronger whole-class conclusion follows from choosing a positive-gap
representative in EACH maximal class under its negation, then applying T1
to the resulting core. It does not require equal outgoing neighborhoods
inside a class, any minimal-counterexample assumption, or symmetrization.

T4. Iterated induced-core lifting (unconditional).
For one representative from each maximal incoming class of H, let K be the
induced representative core. Every pattern certificate of K extends by zero
to a pattern certificate of H. This may be iterated. If some resulting core
has at most five vertices or is a tournament, an original Seymour vertex
lies in the support in that final core. Universal feasibility need only be
proved for cores with distinct, pairwise incomparable incoming neighborhoods.
The arbitrary-chain conclusion does not itself give a whole ORIGINAL
maximal class consisting of Seymour vertices.

T5. Equivalent pattern formulation of SNC (unconditional equivalence).
Every finite oriented core has a nonzero nonnegative pattern certificate
if and only if SNC is true. Infeasibility of a particular finite pattern
system yields, by strict finite minimax and an explicitly described finite
labelled-pattern construction, a genuine finite all-deficient oriented graph.
This theorem does not assert feasibility for arbitrary cores. In particular,
there is no proof of SNC or constructed counterexample in these files.

T6. Full-domain predecessor row map (unconditional surgery).
If rho:V(D)->V(D) satisfies N_D^-(v) subseteq N_D^-(rho(v)), replacing every
outgoing row by the ORIGINAL row of rho(v), simultaneously, produces an
oriented graph D^rho with N++_{D^rho}(v) subseteq N++_D(rho(v)) and
g_{D^rho}(v)>=g_D(rho(v)). Shared heads and modified heads are allowed.
For a single changed row a<-b, equality holds for its second neighborhood,
and every unchanged vertex's second neighborhood can only shrink.

T7. Minimum-order unit-predecessor cover (conditional on a counterexample).
In an all-deficient graph of smallest possible order among ALL such graphs,
for every f some inclusion-maximal incoming-equivalence class C consists
entirely of gap-one vertices pointing to f. Hence at least three maximal
pure-unit classes exist, and the quotient using complete original interclass
arcs has positive minimum indegree and a directed cycle of length at least
three. This is stronger than the existence of an individual unit predecessor.
It is NOT asserted for an arbitrary arc-deletion-minimal counterexample.

T8. Global extremal twin normal form (conditional on a counterexample).
Choose a counterexample by, in order, minimum vertex count, minimum arc count
among ALL counterexamples of that order, maximum sum of gaps, and maximum
sum_F |{v:N_D^+(v)=F}|^2. Incoming containment implies nondecreasing outdegree;
if those degrees coincide, it implies nonincreasing gap. Equal incoming sets
then imply equal outgoing sets, so each incoming-equivalence class is an
independent module with common first and second neighborhoods. The stronger
lexicographic extremal selection is necessary for the stated proof.

Adversarial proof checks
========================
A. Exact distance and source exclusion in T1.
For i in R_H(P_D(v)), an original path c_i->c_j->v exists and c_i->v does
not. If v=c_i, then c_i->c_j and c_j->c_i would be a digon, so i cannot
belong to R_H(P_D(c_i)). The proof does not count the source as a second
target and does not confuse exact distance two with reachability within two.

B. Tournament expansion when the complementary set has zero mass.
Let F=V(H)\(P union R_H(P)). The common outneighbor witness belongs to F,
so F is nonempty even when lambda(F)=0. If lambda(F)>0, summing the skew
game inequalities over F yields 0<=lambda(F)(lambda(R)-lambda(P)). If the
mass is zero, use the inequality at a witness j in F: incoming mass is at
least lambda(P), outgoing mass at most lambda(R). Both cases are valid.

C. Strict duality in T5.
Set a_P(i)=1_{i in R_H(P)}-1_{i in P}. Infeasibility means that the continuous
function min_P lambda dot a_P is negative on the entire compact probability
simplex. Its maximum is therefore strictly negative. Finite minimax supplies
nonnegative pattern weights with every coordinate of their weighted sum
strictly negative. The strict inequalities and rational coefficient matrix
permit rational weights; clear denominators to obtain integer multiplicities
with gamma_i=sum_P w_P(1_{i in P}-1_{i in R_H(P)})>0 for every i.
Thus merely nonpositive duality is NOT being substituted for strict positivity.

D. Realization of the dual pattern weights.
Each pattern vertex v is labelled by j with P(v) subseteq N_H^-(j), and
v->u iff label(v) belongs to P(u). This implies an H arc between the source
and target labels whenever an arc occurs, ruling out loops and digons.
Distinguished full-pattern vertices c_j guarantee ALL necessary two-step
intermediates. Conversely every first intermediate of label j implies the
original core arc i->j, so no additional second-target pattern is introduced.
The source cannot be among its own second targets. Every vertex of label i
therefore has gap K gamma_i+g_H(i), including the distinguished vertices.
For sufficiently large integer K all these gaps are positive. Repeated
patterns and repeated labels do not invalidate any of these arguments.

E. Certificate lifting.
For P subseteq N_H^-(v), choose a maximal-class representative c covering
N_H^-(v). Then P intersect C is a permitted K pattern, and
R_K(P intersect C) subseteq R_H(P) intersect C. Zero extension proves the
required inequality. No completeness, extremality, or closure hypothesis
has been imported.

F. Simultaneous copying.
For a new path v->u->x, original rho(v)->u and incoming containment at u
give original rho(v)->rho(u); the new second arc is original rho(u)->x.
This yields an old exact second target of rho(v). A loop or digon in the
copied graph similarly forces an old loop or digon. The argument works
even when rho(u) is modified and when heads coincide. All rows MUST be
copied from one original snapshot, as specified in the source.

G. Deleting the target after the coupled surgery.
If all original unit predecessors of f can be copied outside their set,
every surviving predecessor of f has gap at least two; unchanged nonunit
predecessors already have that property. Deleting f loses at most one
first neighbor at each such vertex, and deletion cannot create new exact
second targets among the remaining vertices. The smaller graph is therefore
all-deficient. This legitimately uses global minimum order.

H. Extremal hierarchy.
Single-row cloning preserves vertex count and changes the number of arcs
by d+(b)-d+(a). If arc counts tie, other gaps do not decrease. If incoming
sets are equal, both cloning directions are admissible, giving equal
degrees and gaps. Under a tie of the first three objectives, copying into
the larger outgoing-row class strictly increases the fourth objective by
2(r_b-r_a)+2. This argument is sound but must retain ALL four objectives.

Required wording safeguards for the manuscript
==============================================
- State that the five-class theorem is computer-assisted and include the
  exact encoding, complete certificate tables, verifier, and their hashes.
- Distinguish 522,213 permitted patterns including the empty set from
  462,404 nonempty patterns. Both figures are correct; do not interchange them.
- The five-class theorem concerns maximal INCOMING-equivalence classes,
  not maximal protection vertices, arbitrary modules, or total graph order.
- The terminal-core reduction and dual realization are equivalences or
  sufficient conditions, not a resolution of general feasibility.
- State global minimum-order and the stronger four-objective extremality
  locally wherever used; do not merge them into mere arc-set minimality.
- Gap one in the unit-core theorem means g=1, not residual eta=1.
- A graph's chosen representative tournament core guarantees a Seymour
  representative. The whole-class claim is separately justified by choosing
  a positive-gap representative in every class under negation.
- No new priority claim follows from this audit. Fisher is an input for
  density existence; related predecessor-domination and weighted formulations
  still require the manuscript's separate literature comparison.

Overall assessment
==================
These four sources are mathematically coherent and can form a self-contained
main theoretical section, with the small-core certificate tables as a
reproducibility supplement and the extremal surgeries as separate conditional
structure theorems. They do not depend on the earlier residual/median-order
foundation, except where the broader manuscript separately invokes that
foundation. No counterexample or complete proof of SNC has been obtained.
