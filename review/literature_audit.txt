PRIMARY-LITERATURE AND CLAIM-SCOPE AUDIT
Client-date cutoff: 2026-10-07 (Asia/Taipei)
Purpose: editorial support for an unsubmitted SNC manuscript, not a certificate
of originality, acceptance, or a complete worldwide literature search.

RECOMMENDED MANUSCRIPT POSITION

The manuscript proves structural restrictions on stipulated minimal
counterexamples and gives a computer-assisted sufficient condition based on
maximal incoming-neighborhood classes. It does not prove SNC in full. Its
finite incoming-pattern certificates concern arbitrary total graph order,
not merely graphs with at most five vertices. Universal pattern feasibility
is an equivalent reformulation and must not be advertised as progress toward
a solution without an independent method proving feasibility for new cores.

Novelty is unresolved. The exact incoming-core statement, labelled dual
construction, predecessor-containment row copy, and R >= 7 formula were not
identified in the bounded primary sources inspected. Absence from this
search is not evidence that no earlier equivalent result exists.

SPECIFIC COVERAGE CHECK FOR THE FIVE-MAXIMAL-INCOMING-CLASS RESULT

This class is not contained in the hypotheses of the recent Wang--Lu,
Bai--Li--Park, or Brukhman results above. A transparent witness is the
independent equal-m blow-up of the regular five-vertex tournament with
i -> i+1 and i -> i+2 modulo 5, for m >= 4.

There are exactly five distinct inclusion-maximal incoming sets. Its order
is 5m and minimum outdegree is 2m, so it violates the small-outdegree and
n <= 2 delta + 2 assumptions. Its underlying graph is K_{m,m,m,m,m}.
Any independent set lies in one part; its complement contains four full
parts inducing minimum degree 3m, and so is not 3-degenerate. Thus it is not
D_{0,3}. Being in D_{1,1} would make it 4-colorable (each forest is
2-colorable), whereas this graph contains K5. Finally the six distinct
vertices in successive parts 0,1,2,3,4,1 form a directed path of length five
whose endpoints have an arc; the graph is not 5-anti-transitive.

This example itself is already covered by weighted tournament results.
It establishes noncontainment in those SPECIFIC listed classes, not
originality or independence from all previously known methods. Full
comparison with weighted compositions, generalized tournament substitutions,
and neighborhood-domination literature remains open.

For the incoming-pattern/dual equivalence, finite LP duality is standard;
the claimed contribution is the precise restricted pattern system and the
labelled realization preserving exact second neighborhoods. Include that
construction, the diagonal/no-digon checks, and a scope statement. Do not
call an equivalent reformulation a proof of any infeasible core's existence.
